# Unit 36: Adverbs of Frequency

## Learning Objectives
- Know the list of adverbs of frequency from always (100%) to never (0%)
- Place adverbs of frequency before the main verb
- Place adverbs of frequency after the verb to be

## Adverbs of Frequency

We use adverbs of frequency to say if something happens often or not.

| Adverb | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|
| always | 100% |
| usually | ~80% |
| often | ~60% |
| sometimes | ~40% |
| hardly ever | ~10% |
| never | 0% |

## Position Rules

The usual position for adverbs of frequency is **before the main verb**. Adverbs of frequency go **after the verb to be**.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Before main verb | I always do my homework. |
| Before main verb | I usually go to the theatre. |
| After verb to be | I am often relaxed. |
| After verb to be | I am sometimes late for class. |
| Before main verb | I hardly ever go out with my friends. |
| Before main verb | I never dance. |
