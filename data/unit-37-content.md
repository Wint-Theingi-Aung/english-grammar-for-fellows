# Unit 37: Prepositions of Time

## Learning Objectives
- Use "in" with parts of the day, months, and years
- Use "on" with days and dates
- Use "at" with times, night, and the weekend

## In

Use "in" with:
- Parts of the day: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
- Months: in September, in December
- Years: in 1990, in 2021

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Parts of the day | I study Spanish in the evening. |
| Months | They start school in June. |
| Years | My father died in 1987. |

## On

Use "on" with:
- Days: on Monday, on Sunday
- Dates: on 20th December, on 14th July

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Days | Can you come on Monday? |
| Dates | Schools start on 7th September. |

## At

Use "at" with:
- Times: at six o'clock, at ten to eleven
- Night: at night
- The weekend: at the weekend

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Times | We start school at half past eight. |
| Night | We do homework at night. |
| The weekend | She wants to go to school at the weekend. |
