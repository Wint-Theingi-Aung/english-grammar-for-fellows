# Unit 1: Simple Present Tense, Simple Past Tense and Simple Future Tense

## Learning Objectives
- Understand and use Simple Present Tense, Simple Past Tense, and Simple Future Tense
- Form affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences
- Use the correct verb forms for different subjects (I, we, you, he, she, it, they)
- Understand when to use each tense

## Verb Forms Overview

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Simple Present | V1 (base form) | I write a letter. |
| Simple Past | V2 (past form) | I wrote a letter last night. |
| Simple Future | will + V1 | I will write a letter next week. |

## Subject-Verb Conjugation Table

| Subject | Simple Present | Simple Past | Simple Future |
|---------|----------------|-------------|---------------|
| I | write | wrote | will write |
| We | go | went | will go |
| You | swim | swam | will swim |
| He | love | loved | will love |
| She | know | knew | will know |
| It | rain | rained | will rain |
| They | live | lived | will live |

## Example Sentences

### Simple Present Tense
- I write a letter. (write = verb root)
  - ကျွန်မ စာတစ်စောင် ရေးတယ်။
- We go to the park every Sunday.
- You swim in the river.
- He loves his cat.
- She knows me.
- It rains.
- They live in Yangon.

### Simple Past Tense
- I wrote a letter last night.
  - မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ စာတစ်စောင် ရေးခဲ့တယ်။
- We went to the park last Sunday.
- You swam in the river yesterday.
- He loved his cat when he was young.
- She knew me.
- It rained five minutes ago.
- They lived in Yangon three years ago.

### Simple Future Tense
- I will write a letter next week.
  - နောက်အပတ် ကျွန်မ စာတစ်စောင် ရေးမယ်။
- We will go to the park next month.
- You will swim in the river tomorrow.
- He will love his cat if the cat loves him.
- She will know me.
- It will rain tomorrow morning.
- They will live in Yangon next year.

## Sentence Forms

"Do" is used as an auxiliary verb to make negative and question sentences of Simple Present Tense and Simple Past Tense. In Simple Future Tense, we use will + V1.

### Affirmative, Negative, Interrogative, and Negative Interrogative Forms

#### Simple Present Tense

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I write a letter. | I don't write a letter. | Do I write a letter? | Don't I write a letter? |
| ကျွန်မ စာရေးတယ်။ | ကျွန်မ စာ မရေးဘူး။ | ကျွန်မ စာ ရေးလား။ | ကျွန်မ စာ မရေးဘူးလား။ |

#### Simple Past Tense

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I wrote a letter last night. | I didn't write a letter last night. | Did I write a letter last night? | Didn't I write a letter last night? |
| မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ စာတစ်စောင် ရေးခဲ့တယ်။ | မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ စာ မရေးခဲ့ဘူး။ | မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ စာ ရေးခဲ့လား။ | မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ စာ မရေးခဲ့ဘူးလား။ |

#### Simple Future Tense

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I will write a letter next week. | I won't write a letter next week. | Will I write a letter next week? | Won't I write a letter next week? |
| နောက်အပတ်ကျရင် ကျွန်မ စာတစ်စောင်ရေးမယ်။ | နောက်အပတ်ကျရင် ကျွန်မ စာ ရေးဖို့မ မဟုတ်ဘူး။ | နောက်အပတ်ကျရင် ကျွန်မ စာ ရေးဖို့မ လား။ | နောက်အပတ်ကျရင် ကျွန်မ စာ ရေးဖို့မ မဟုတ်ဘူး မလား။ |

## Answer Forms (Short and Long Answers)

When we are replying Yes/No Questions, we can use short or long form for the answer.

### Examples:

**Do you write a letter?**
- Short Answer: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Long Answer: Yes, I write a letter. / No, I don't write a letter.

**Did you write a letter last night?**
- Short Answer: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, I wrote a letter last night. / No, I didn't write a letter last night.

**Will you write a letter next week?**
- Short Answer: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
- Long Answer: Yes, I will write a letter next week. / No, I won't write a letter next week.

**Do we go to the park every Sunday?**
- Short Answer: Yes, we do. / No, we don't.
- Long Answer: Yes, we go to the park every Sunday. / No, we don't go to the park every Sunday.

**Did we go to the park?**
- Short Answer: Yes, we did. / No, we didn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, we went to the park. / No, we didn't go to the park.

**Will we go to the park next month?**
- Short Answer: Yes, we will. / No, we won't.
- Long Answer: Yes, we will go to the park next month. / No, we won't go to the park next month.

**Does she know me?**
- Short Answer: Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, she knows you. / No, she doesn't know you.

**Did she know me?**
- Short Answer: Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, she knew you. / No, she didn't know you.

**Will she know me?**
- Short Answer: Yes, she will. / No, she won't.
- Long Answer: Yes, she will know you. / No, she won't know you.

**Does he live in Yangon?**
- Short Answer: Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, he lives in Yangon. / No, he doesn't live in Yangon.

**Did he live in Yangon three years ago?**
- Short Answer: Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, he lived in Yangon three years ago. / No, he didn't live in Yangon three years ago.

**Will he live in Yangon next year?**
- Short Answer: Yes, he will. / No, he won't.
- Long Answer: Yes, he will live in Yangon next year. / No, he won't live in Yangon next year.
