# Unit 18: There was / There were

## Learning Objectives
- Use "there was" and "there were" to talk about things that existed in the past
- Use "there wasn't" and "there weren't" to say things didn't exist
- Form past tense questions with "Was there" and "Were there"
- Ask about past quantities with "How many" and "How much"

## There was / There were (Past)

| Form | Usage |
|------|-------|
| There was + singular noun | Talk about one thing in the past |
| There wasn't + singular noun | Say one thing didn't exist in the past |
| There was + uncountable noun | Talk about uncountable things in the past |
| There wasn't + uncountable noun | Say uncountable things didn't exist |
| There were + plural noun | Talk about many things in the past |
| There weren't + plural noun | Say many things didn't exist |

## Key Rules

| Rule | Example | Burmese |
|------|---------|---------|
| There was + singular noun | There was a cinema. | ရုပ်ရှင်ရုံတစ်ခု ရှိခဲ့တယ်။ |
| There wasn't + singular noun | There wasn't a cinema. | ရုပ်ရှင်ရုံတစ်ခု မရှိခဲ့ဘူး။ |
| There was + uncountable noun | There was some milk. | နွားနို့ အနည်းငယ် ရှိခဲ့တယ်။ |
| There weren't + plural noun | There weren't any books. | စာအုပ်တွေ မရှိခဲ့ဘူး။ |
| How many + plural + were there? | How many dogs were there in the park? | ပန်းခြံထဲမှာ ခွေး ဘယ်နှစ်ကောင် ရှိခဲ့လဲ။ |
| How much + uncountable + was there? | How much money was there in your bank account? | မင်းဘဏ်အကောင့်ထဲမှာ ငွေ ဘယ်လောက် ရှိခဲ့လဲ။ |
