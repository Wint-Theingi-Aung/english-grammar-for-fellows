# Unit 10: Must

## Learning Objectives
- Understand when to use "must" in English sentences
- Use must for strong personal obligations
- Use mustn't to express prohibition
- Understand that the past form of "must" is "had to"

## Must Overview

| Modal | Form | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|
| Must | Subject + must + V1 | Strong personal obligations (present/future) |
| Mustn't | Subject + mustn't + V1 | Prohibition (something is not allowed) |

## Use 1: Must for Strong Obligation

"Must" is used for strong personal obligations. It expresses something that is necessary or very important.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I must lose some weight. | ငါ ဝိတ်ချရမယ်။ |
| I must write a letter to John. | ငါ ဂျွန်ဆီ စာတစ်စောင် ရေးရမယ်။ |
| Bicyclists must remember to signal when they turn. | စက်ဘီးစီးသူများ လှည့်တဲ့အခါ အမှတ်ပေးဖို့ မှတ်ထားရမယ်။ |
| He must study harder if he wants to pass the exam. | သူ စာမေးပွဲ အောင်ချင်ရင် ပိုပြီး ကြိုးစားရမယ်။ |

## Use 2: Mustn't for Prohibition

"Mustn't" (must not) is used to express that something is prohibited or not allowed.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| Passengers mustn't talk to the driver. | ခရီးသည်များ ဒီရင်ဘောက် စကားမပြောခင်းမရပါ။ |
| Policemen mustn't drink on duty. | ရဲတပ်ဖွဲ့ဝင်များ တာဝန်ထမ်းဆောင်နေချိန်မှာ အရက်မသောက်ရပါ။ |
| Children mustn't eat chewing gum. | ကလေးများ ဝါဂွမ်းမစားရပါ။ |
| They mustn't use their mobile phones during lessons. | သူတို့ သင်ခန်းစာချိန်မှာ မိုဘိုင်းဖုန်းများ အသုံးမပြုရပါ။ |

## Use 3: Must in Questions

"Must" can be used in questions to ask about obligation.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| Must I go to the party? | ကျွန်တော် ပါတီသွားရမလား။ |
| Must he make so much noise? | သူ ဆူညံစွာ လုပ်ရမလား။ |

## Important Notes
- "Must" is used only in the present and future
- The past form of "must" is "had to"
- "Must" does not exist in the past tense
