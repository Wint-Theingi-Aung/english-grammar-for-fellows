# Unit 7: Should / Ought to

## Learning Objectives
- Understand when to use "should" and "ought to" in English sentences
- Form affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences with should/ought to
- Use should and ought to for advice, expectations, and obligations
- Understand the difference between should and ought to

## Should / Ought to Overview

| Modal | Form | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|
| Should | Subject + should + V1 | Advice, expectation, mild obligation |
| Ought to | Subject + ought to + V1 | Advice, expectation, stronger obligation |

## Use of Should

"Should" is used to give advice, express expectations, or indicate what is right or wrong.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| You shouldn't drink alcohol. | မင်း အရက်မသောက်သင့်ဘူး။ |
| Should I go to the temple on Thursday? | ကျွန်မ ကြာသပတေးနေ့ ဘုရားကျောင်း သွားသင့်လား။ |
| There is a traffic jam on this road, you should go by Metro. | ဒီလမ်းပေါ်မှာ ယာဉ်ကြောပိတ်ဆို့မှု ဖြစ်နေတယ်။ မင်း မီတရို စီးသင့်တယ်။ |

## Use of Ought to

"Ought to" is similar to "should" but can express a slightly stronger obligation or moral duty.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| You ought to respect your elders. | မင်း အသက်ကြီးသူတွေကို လေးစားသင့်တယ်။ |
| You oughtn't to ride a motorbike without a helmet. | မင်း ဦးထုပ်မပါဘဲ ဆိုင်ကယ်မစီးသင့်ဘူး။ |
| Ought she to call the police? | သူမ ရဲကို ခေါ်သင့်လား။ |

## Sentence Forms

### Should

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| You should go. | You shouldn't go. | Should you go? | Shouldn't you go? |
| He should study. | He shouldn't study. | Should he study? | Shouldn't he study? |

### Ought to

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| You ought to go. | You ought not to go. | Ought you to go? | Ought you not to go? |
| He ought to study. | He ought not to study. | Ought he to study? | Ought he not to study? |

## Key Differences

| Feature | Should | Ought to |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Formality | Less formal | More formal |
| Strength | Softer advice | Stronger obligation |
| Negative | shouldn't | ought not to |
| Question | Should + subject? | Ought + subject + to? |
