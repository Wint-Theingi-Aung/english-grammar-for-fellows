# Unit 43: Active and Passive Voice

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the difference between Active Voice and Passive Voice
- Convert Active Voice to Passive Voice in Simple Present, Simple Past, Simple Future
- Convert Active Voice to Passive Voice in Present Continuous, Past Continuous
- Convert Active Voice to Passive Voice in Present Perfect, Past Perfect, Future Perfect

## Definition
Active Voice means that a sentence has a subject that acts upon its verb. Passive Voice means that the subject is a recipient of a verb's action.

## Simple Present Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| Reporters write news reports. | News reports are written by Reporters. |
| He helps John. | John is helped. |
| Teachers teach students. | Students are taught by teachers. |
| I don't write a letter. | A letter is not written. |
| I don't abuse my servants. | My servants are not abused. |

## Simple Past Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| The company hired new workers last year. | New workers were hired by the company last year. |
| He wrote a letter. | A letter was written. |
| Sue loved John very much. | John was loved by Sue very much. |
| She didn't write a story. | A story was not written. |
| John didn't eat bananas yesterday. | Bananas were not eaten by John yesterday. |

## Simple Future Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| The company will hire new workers. | New workers will be hired by the company. |
| Someone will finish the work by 5:00 PM. | The work will be finished by 5:00 PM. |
| John will complete a job tomorrow. | A job will be completed by John tomorrow. |
| She will not hate us. | We will not be hated. |
| Susan will not take medicine. | Medicine will not be taken by Susan. |

## Present Continuous Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| A robot is serving food in this hotel. | Food is being served by a robot in this hotel. |
| John is taking interviews for different posts. | Interviews are being taken for different posts by John. |
| James is teaching English at this university. | English is being taught by James at this university. |
| Rahul is not training the kids. | The kids are not being trained by Rahul. |
| She is not playing games. | Games are not being played. |

## Past Continuous Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| She was writing a novel. | A novel was being written. |
| I was taking photos. | Photos were being taken. |
| He was giving a lecture. | A lecture was being given. |
| She was not preparing dinner. | Dinner was not being prepared. |
| Sue was not washing clothes. | Clothes were not being washed by Sue. |

## Present Perfect Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| They have already discussed the book. | The book has already been discussed. |
| I have taken medicine. | Medicine has been taken. |
| James has eaten apples. | Apples have been eaten by James. |
| I have not gone to Yangon. | Yangon has not been gone to. |
| Sue has not written novels. | Novels have not been written by Sue. |

## Past Perfect Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| He had delivered the letters. | The letters had been delivered. |
| John had sung a song. | A song had been sung by John. |
| Susan had taught English at the university. | English had been taught by Susan at the university. |
| I had not gone to Yangon. | Yangon had not been gone to. |
| Sue had not written novels. | Novels had not been written by Sue. |

## Future Perfect Tense
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| I will have written a letter. | A letter will have been written. |
| She will have made cake. | Cake will have been made. |
| They won't have bought laptops. | Laptops won't have been bought. |
| We will have taken the test. | The test will have been taken. |
| The children will have seen the show. | The show will have been seen by the children. |
