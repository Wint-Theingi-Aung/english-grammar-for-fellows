# Unit 32: If … not ↔ Unless

## Learning Objectives
- Understand that "If … not" and "Unless" have the same meaning ("except if")
- Convert between "If … not" and "Unless" structures
- Remember that "will/would" is not used directly after "unless"

## If … not ↔ Unless

"If … not" and "Unless" have the same meaning: "except if".

### Pattern: If + not + clause ↔ Unless + clause

| If … not | Unless |
|----------|--------|
| If it doesn't rain tonight, the match will start at 9 o'clock tomorrow. | Unless it rains tonight, the match will start at 9 o'clock tomorrow. |
| If you don't know the way, don't dare to walk ahead. | Unless you know the way, don't dare to walk ahead. |

### Converting Unless → If … not

| Unless | If … not |
|--------|----------|
| Unless John wears a sweater, he will catch a cold. | If John doesn't wear a sweater, he will catch a cold. |
| You will fail unless you study hard. | You will fail if you don't study hard. |

## Attention
Unless is like "if" in that we do not use "will" or "would" directly after it.

- If you violate the rules, you will be in trouble. ✓
- Unless you violate the rules, you will not be in trouble. ✓
- ~~Unless you will violate the rules, you will be in trouble.~~ ✗
