# Unit 19: Although / In spite of

## Learning Objectives
- Use "although" to show contrast between two clauses
- Use "in spite of" to show contrast before a noun or Ving
- Understand the difference in structure between "although" and "in spite of"

## Although / In spite of

| Form | Usage |
|------|-------|
| Although + subject + verb | Show contrast between two clauses |
| In spite of + noun / Ving | Show contrast before a noun or gerund |

## Key Rules

| Rule | Example | Burmese |
|------|---------|---------|
| Although + subject + verb | Although she ran very fast, she lost the race. | သူမ အမြန်ပြေးပေမယ့် ပြိုင်ပွဲမှာ ရှုံးသွားတယ်။ |
| Although + clause | Although it rained all day, we enjoyed the party very much. | မိုးတစ်နေ့လုံး ရွာခဲ့ပေမယ့် ငါတို့ ပါတီကို ပျော်ရွှင်စွာ ဖြတ်သန်းခဲ့တယ်။ |
| Although + clause (end) | They are generous although they are poor. | သူတို့ ဆင်းရဲပေမယ့် ပေးကမ်းစွန့်ကြဲတတ်ကြတယ်။ |
| In spite of + noun | In spite of their quarrel, they remain the best of friends. | ရန်ဖြစ်ထားပေမယ့်ပဲ အကောင်းဆုံး သငယ်ချင်းဖြစ်နေကြတန်ပါ။ |
| In spite of + noun | We went out in spite of the rain. | မိုးရွာပေမယ့်ပဲ ငါတို့ အပြင်ထွက်ခဲ့တယ်။ |
| In spite of + Ving | In spite of completing his master's degree, he could not find a quality job. | မောစတာဘွဲ့ရပေမယ့်ပဲ သူ အလုပ်ကောင်းကောင်း မရခဲ့ဘူး။ |
