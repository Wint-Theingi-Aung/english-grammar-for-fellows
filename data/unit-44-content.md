# Unit 44: Reported Speech

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the concept of reported speech
- Learn tense changes when converting direct speech to reported speech
- Learn pronoun and place/time word changes
- Practice converting direct speech to reported speech in various contexts

## What is Reported Speech?
Reported speech is when we tell someone what another person said. When converting direct speech to reported speech, tenses shift back.

## Tense Changes in Reported Speech

### Simple Present → Simple Past
Direct Speech ရဲ့ Simple Present Tense ကို Reported Speech မှာ Simple Past Tense ပြောင်းပါတယ်။

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| She said, "I work at home." | She said that she worked at home. |
| He said, "I like this song." | He said that he liked that song. |

### Simple Past → Past Perfect
Direct Speech ရဲ့ Simple Past Tense ကို Reported Speech မှာ Past Perfect Tense ပြောင်းပါတယ်။

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| John said to mother, "You made my day..." | John told mother that she had made his day... |
| Sue said, "I was very happy." | She said that she had been very happy. |
| The film began at seven o'clock. | He said that the film had begun at seven o'clock. |

### Modal Verbs
Modal Verbs တွေလည်း ပြောင်းပါတယ်။ will→would, can→could, may→might, must/have to→had to

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| I will work. | She said she would work. |
| I can work. | She said she could work. |
| I may come. | She said she might come. |
| I must go. | She said she had to go. |

### Present Continuous → Past Continuous
Present Continuous ကို Past Continuous ပြောင်းပါတယ်။

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| I am working. | She said that she was working. |
| They are busy. | They said that they were busy. |

### Present Perfect → Past Perfect
Present Perfect ကို Past Perfect ပြောင်းပါတယ်။

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| She has finished her dance practice. | Adele said that she had finished her dance practice. |

### Past Perfect
Past Perfect ကတော့ မပြောင်းပါဘူး။

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| I had worked. | She said she had worked. |

## Pronoun and Place/Time Changes

In reported speech, pronouns and place and time words also change.

### Pronoun Changes
Pronoun တွေ ပြောင်းပါတယ်။ I→he/she, We→they, my→his/her, your→my, our→their

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| I → he/she | my → his/her |
| We → they | your → my |
| our → their | me → him/her |
| us → them | |

### Place and Time Word Changes
Place နဲ့ Time words တွေ ပြောင်းပါတယ်။ this→that, here→there, today→that day, yesterday→the day before, tomorrow→the next day

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| this/these → that/those | here → there |
| today → that day | yesterday → the day before |
| tomorrow → the next/following day | last night → the night before |
| next → the following | last → the previous |
| ago → before | |

## Examples with Changes

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| She said, "I am not hungry now." | She said she was not hungry then. |
| She said, "It is very quiet here." | She said it was very quiet there. |
| She said, "I went to the cinema yesterday." | She said that she had gone to the cinema the day before. |

## Reporting Questions

### Yes/No Questions with "if"
| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "Is it your birthday today?" Beth asked Nathan. | Beth asked Nathan if it was his birthday that day. |
| He asked, "Do I have to do it?" | He asked if he had to do it. |
| "Are you crazy?" she asked him. | She asked him if he was crazy. |

### Wh-Questions
| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "Where is your sister?" she asked me. | She asked me where my sister was. |
| "How are you?" Martin asked us. | Martin asked us how we were. |
| "Which dresses do you like best?" she asked her boyfriend. | She asked her boyfriend which dresses he liked best. |
| The teacher asked, "Who speaks English?" | The teacher wanted to know who spoke English. |
| "What's the time?" he asked. | He wanted to know what the time was. |
| "Why don't you help me?" she asked him. | She wanted to know why he didn't help her. |

## Reporting Commands and Requests

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "Don't play on the grass, boys," she said. | She told the boys not to play on the grass. |
| "Say hello to Jim," they said. | They asked me to say hello to Jim. |
| "Be careful, Ben," she said. | She told Ben to be careful. |
| She said, "Go upstairs." | She told me to go upstairs. |
| "Close the door behind you," he told me. | He told me to close the door behind me. |
| "Stop staring at me," she said. | She told him to stop staring at her. |
| "Leave me alone," she said. | She told me to leave her alone. |
| "John, stop smoking," she said. | She told John to stop smoking. |
