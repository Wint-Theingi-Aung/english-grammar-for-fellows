# Unit 5: Wh Questions

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the difference between Wh-questions and Yes/No questions
- Learn all Wh-question words: who, what, when, where, why, which, how, whom, whose
- Form Wh-questions correctly using the pattern: Wh-word + auxiliary + subject + main verb?
- Use the correct Wh-word for different types of information

## Types of Questions

There are two main types of questions in English:
1. **Yes/No Questions** — Questions that can be answered with "Yes" or "No"
2. **Wh-Questions** — Open questions that require specific information as an answer

## Wh-Question Words

### Who — To ask about people (subject)
- Who are you?
  - မင်းက ဘယ်သူလဲ။
- Who is she?
  - သူမက ဘယ်သူလဲ။
- Who did it?
  - ဘယ်သူ လုပ်တာလဲ။
- Who made this cake?
  - ဘယ်သူက ဒီကိတ်ကို လုပ်တာလဲ။

### What — To ask about something
- What's that?
  - ဒါက ဘာလဲ။
- What's his name?
  - သူ့နာမည်က ဘာလဲ။
- What day is it today?
  - ဒီနေ့က ဘယ်နေ့လဲ။
- What are you doing?
  - မင်း ဘာလုပ်နေတာလဲ။
- What do you do?
  - မင်း ဘာအလုပ်လုပ်တာလဲ။

### When — To ask about time
- When is your birthday?
  - မင်း မွေးနေ့က ဘယ်အချိန်လဲ။
- When were you born?
  - မင်း ဘယ်အချိန် မွေးတာလဲ။
- When does the train arrive?
  - ဘူတာကား ဘယ်အချိန် ရောက်မလဲ။
- When will you finish work?
  - မင်း ဘယ်အချိန် အလုပ်ပြီးမလဲ။

### Where — To ask about place
- Where is my pencil case?
  - ငါ့ဘေးပင်အိတ်က ဘယ်မှာလဲ။
- Where are you from?
  - မင်း ဘယ်ကလဲ။
- Where do you eat lunch?
  - မင်း နေ့လည်စာ ဘယ်မှာ စားတာလဲ။
- Where do you work?
  - မင်း ဘယ်မှာ အလုပ်လုပ်တာလဲ။

### Why — To ask the reason
- Why are you so happy?
  - ဘာကြောင့် ဒီလောက်ပျော်နေတာလဲ။
- Why did you do that?
  - ဘာကြောင့် ဒါကို လုပ်တာလဲ။
- Why do we need to study English?
  - ဘာကြောင့် အင်္ဂလိပ်စာ သင်ဖို့ လိုအပ်တာလဲ။
- Why were you late?
  - ဘာကြောင့် နောက်ကျတာလဲ။

### Which — To ask about choices
- Which way is it to the library?
  - စာကြည့်တိုက်က ဘယ်လမ်း သွားရမလဲ။
- Which restaurant shall we go to?
  - ငါတို့ ဘယ်စားသောက်ဆိုင် သွားရမလဲ။
- Which country do you want to visit?
  - မင်း ဘယ်နိုင်ငံကို သွားချင်တာလဲ။
- Which one is it?
  - ဒါက ဘယ်ဟာလဲ။

### How — To ask about manner/condition/quantity
- How does this work?
  - ဒါက ဘယ်လို အလုပ်လုပ်တာလဲ။
- How are you?
  - မင်း ဘယ်လိုနေလဲ။
- How was your vacation?
  - မင်း အားလပ်ရက် ဘယ်လို ဖြတ်သန်းခဲ့လဲ။
- How far is the restaurant?
  - စားသောက်ဆိုင်က ဘယ်လောက် ဝေးလဲ။
- How long will it take?
  - ဘယ်လောက် ကြာမလဲ။
- How many pencils are there?
  - ဘေးပင် ဘယ်နှစ်ချောင်း ရှိလဲ။
- How much money do you have?
  - မင်းမှာ ဘယ်လောက် ငွေရှိလဲ။
- How old are you?
  - မင်း အသက် ဘယ်နှစ်လဲ။

### Whom — To ask about people (object)
- Whom did you see?
  - မင်း ဘယ်သူ့ကို တွေ့တာလဲ။
- Whom did you give money?
  - မင်း ဘယ်သူ့ကို ငွေပေးတာလဲ။
- Whom are you calling?
  - မင်း ဘယ်သူ့ကို ခေါ်နေတာလဲ။

### Whose — To ask about possession
- Whose pencil is this?
  - ဒီဘေးပင်က ဘယ်သူ့ဟာလဲ။
- Whose books are these?
  - ဒီစာအုပ်တွေက ဘယ်သူ့ဟာလဲ။
- Whose house is this?
  - ဒီအိမ်က ဘယ်သူ့ဟာလဲ။

## Rules for Forming Wh-Questions

The basic pattern for Wh-questions is:

**Wh-word + auxiliary verb + subject + main verb?**

| Wh-word | Auxiliary | Subject | Main Verb | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|-----------|---------|
| Where | do | you | live | Where do you live? |
| Who | is | she | — | Who is she? |
| What | does | he | write | What does he write? |
| When | will | you | finish | When will you finish? |
| Why | are | you | late | Why are you late? |
| How | do | you | go to school | How do you go to school? |
| Which | one | do | you like | Which one do you like? |
| Whose | book | is | this | Whose book is this? |

### Special Notes:
- **Who** is used as the subject: Who likes soccer? (no auxiliary needed)
- **How** combines with adjectives/adverbs: How many, How much, How old, How far, How long
- **Whose** asks about possession and is followed by a noun: Whose book is this?
