# Unit 49: Vocabulary — Topic-Based Lessons

Unit 49 is a vocabulary unit covering 31 topic-based lessons. Each lesson focuses on a specific vocabulary topic with word lists, meanings, and practice exercises.

## Lessons

**Vocabulary:** art, biology, chemistry, English, French, geography, German, history, ICT (Information and Communication Technology), maths, music, PE (Physical Education), physics, Spanish, go to school (by bike/bus/car/train/on foot), have lunch, have a break, get up, do homework, go to bed, start school, get dressed, have dinner, finish school, have breakfast, go home
