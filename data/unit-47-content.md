# Unit 47: Proverbs

## Learning Objectives
- Learn 55 common English proverbs and their meanings
- Understand the wisdom and advice conveyed by each proverb
- Recognize proverbs in context and use them appropriately

## Proverbs

| # | Proverb | Meaning |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | A leopard cannot change its spots. | People cannot change their fundamental nature. |
| 2 | A fool and his money are soon parted. | A foolish person quickly loses their money. |
| 3 | A barking dog seldom bites. | People who make loud threats rarely carry them out. |
| 4 | A mouse may help a lion. | Even small or weak beings can help the powerful. |
| 5 | A friend to all is a friend to none. | Someone who tries to be friends with everyone truly has no real friends. |
| 6 | All that glitters is not gold. (Appearances can be deceptive.) | Something that looks valuable or attractive may not be so. |
| 7 | All covet, all lose. | Greed causes people to lose everything. |
| 8 | Blood is thicker than water. | Family relationships are stronger than other relationships. |
| 9 | Better late than never. | It is better to do something late than not at all. |
| 10 | Bad news travels fast. | Negative information spreads quickly. |
| 11 | Charity begins at home. | One should take care of family first before helping others. |
| 12 | Don't look a gift horse in the mouth. | Do not criticize or find fault with something given to you for free. |
| 13 | Don't put all your eggs in one basket. | Do not risk everything on a single plan or opportunity. |
| 14 | Don't judge a book by its cover. | Do not judge something or someone by outward appearance alone. |
| 15 | Easier said than done. | Something is much harder to do than to talk about. |
| 16 | Empty vessels make the greatest song. | People with little knowledge often talk the most. |
| 17 | Every cloud has a silver lining. | There is something good in every bad situation. |
| 18 | Easy come, easy go. | What is gained easily is lost easily. |
| 19 | Familiarity breeds contempt. | Too much closeness leads to a lack of respect. |
| 20 | Fools are wise as long as silent. | A fool seems wise when they do not speak. |
| 21 | Gardens are not made by sitting in the shade. | Success requires effort and hard work. |
| 22 | Grasp all, lose all. | If you try to hold on to everything, you will lose everything. |
| 23 | Hatred is as blind as love. | Hatred, like love, can cloud a person's judgment. |
| 24 | Ignorance is bliss. | Not knowing something can sometimes make you happier. |
| 25 | If you want a friend, be a friend. | To have friends, you must first be a good friend. |
| 26 | Keep something for a rainy day. | Save resources for a time of need. |
| 27 | Kill the goose that lays the golden eggs. | Destroy something that produces valuable results for you. |
| 28 | Living well is the best revenge. | The best response to those who wrong you is to live happily. |
| 29 | Look after number one. | Take care of yourself first. |
| 30 | Lend only that which you can afford to lose. | Only lend what you can afford not to get back. |
| 31 | Make hay while the sun shines. | Take advantage of favorable conditions while they last. |
| 32 | Might is right. | The powerful determine what is considered right. |
| 33 | Mind your own business. | Do not interfere in other people's affairs. |
| 34 | More haste, less speed. | Going too fast leads to mistakes and slows you down. |
| 35 | One rotten apple spoils the whole bunch. | One bad person can negatively influence an entire group. |
| 36 | Opportunity seldom knocks twice. | Good opportunities are rare and should be seized. |
| 37 | Out of sight, out of mind. | When something is not seen, it is easily forgotten. |
| 38 | Prevention is better than cure. | It is better to stop something bad from happening than to fix it later. |
| 39 | Rome was not built in a day. | Important achievements take time and patience. |
| 40 | Solitude is often the best company. | Being alone can be more enjoyable than being with others. |
| 41 | The longest night will have an end. | Even the most difficult times will eventually pass. |
| 42 | The pen is mightier than the sword. | Words and ideas have more influence than violence. |
| 43 | The last straw breaks the camel's back. | A small final burden causes something to fail. |
| 44 | The early bird catches the worm. | Success comes to those who act early and prepare well. |
| 45 | Time cures all ills. | Given enough time, problems and suffering will fade. |
| 46 | There is a time to speak and a time to be silent. | It is important to know when to talk and when to stay quiet. |
| 47 | There is truth in wine. | People reveal their true feelings when they are drunk. |
| 48 | When in Rome do as Romans do. | When in a new place, follow the customs of the people there. |
| 49 | What will be, will be. (You cannot avoid fate or change the way that things are.) | The future is determined and cannot be changed. |
| 50 | What's done is done. (You cannot change what has already happened. You cannot put back the clock.) | The past cannot be changed, so move on. |
| 51 | Words have wings and cannot be recalled. | Once spoken, words cannot be taken back. |
| 52 | Words cut more than swords. | Hurtful words cause deeper wounds than physical weapons. |
| 53 | You can't take it with you when you die. | Material wealth cannot be taken to the afterlife. |
| 54 | You can't please everyone. | It is impossible to satisfy every person. |
| 55 | You can't make an omelette without breaking egg. | Achieving something worthwhile requires sacrifice. |
