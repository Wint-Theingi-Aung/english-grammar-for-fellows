# Unit 38: Prepositions of Place

## Learning Objectives
- Use "on" to indicate a position above but touching the object
- Use "under" to talk about something below or lower than something else
- Use "above" to indicate a much higher position than "on"
- Use "near" to express "not far away in distance"
- Use "behind" to indicate the back part of something
- Use "in front of" to indicate a position facing someone/something
- Use "next to" to indicate very close to someone or something
- Use "in" to indicate inside an area

## On

Use "on" to indicate a position above but touching the object.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Above but touching | There's a cat on the desk. |

## Under

Use "under" to talk about something that is below or lower than something else.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Below or lower than | The dog usually sits under the table, on your feet! |

## Above

Use "above" to indicate a much higher position than the preposition "on" does.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Much higher position | We have a new light above the dining table. |

## Near

Use "near" to express "not far away in distance".

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Not far away | My house is near my school – only two minutes away. |

## Behind

Use "behind" to indicate the back part of something. "Behind" is opposite of "in front of".

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| At the back of | Susie is standing behind the tree so I can't see her. |

## In front of

Use "in front of" to indicate a position facing someone/something.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Facing someone/something | The teacher usually stands in front of the class. |

## Next to

Use "next to" to indicate very close to someone or something.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Very close to | The lamp is next to the sofa. |

## In

Use "in" to indicate inside an area.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Inside an area | The fish is in the bowl. |
