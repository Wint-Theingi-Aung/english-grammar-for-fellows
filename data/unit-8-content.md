# Unit 8: Can / Could

## Learning Objectives
- Understand when to use "can" and "could" in English sentences
- Form affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences with can/could
- Use can for present/future ability and informal permission
- Use could for past ability, polite requests, and formal permission

## Can / Could Overview

| Modal | Form | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|
| Can | Subject + can + V1 | Present/future ability, informal permission, possibility |
| Could | Subject + could + V1 | Past ability, polite requests, formal permission, possibility |

## Use 1: Can for Present/Future Ability (Informal)

"Can" is used to talk about the ability to do something at a given time in the present or future. It is used to make statements about what is possible. "Can" is more informal.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| He can speak Chinese fluently. | သူ အင်္ဂလိပ်စာကို ကောင်းကောင်းကျွမ်းကျင်စွာ ပြောနိုင်တယ်။ |
| You can take a holiday tomorrow if you want. | မင်းနားချင်ရင် မနက်ဖန်နားနိုင်တယ်။ |
| I cannot drink too much. | ငါအရမ်းများများ မသောက်နိုင်ဘူး။ |
| Can you take me to the Victoria station? | မင်း ငါ့ကို ဗစ်တိုးရီးယား ရထားဘူတာဆီ ခေါ်သွားပေးနိုင်မလား။ |
| Can I borrow your car tomorrow? | မင်းရဲ့ကားကို မနက်ဖန် ငါ ငှားရမ်းနိုင်မလား။ |

## Use 2: Could for Past Ability and Polite Requests

"Could" is used to talk about past time ability. "Could" is also used as a polite way of telling or asking to do something. "Could" is more formal and polite than "can".

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I could run 10 miles when I was young. | ငါငယ်ငယ်က ၁၀ မိုင် ပြေးနိုင်ခဲ့တယ်။ |
| I could not take chilli when I was 12. | ကျွန်မ ၁၂ နှစ်တုန်းက ငရုတ်သီး မစားနိုင်ခဲ့ဘူး။ |
| Could you lock the door when you leave? | မင်းထွက်သွားရင် တံခါးခတ်ပေးနိုင်မလား။ |
| Could I choose my own doctor? | ကျွန်တော်တို့အတွက်ဆရာဝန်ကို ကျွန်တော်တို့ဘက်က ကိုယ်တိုင် လွတ်လပ်စွာ ရွေးချယ်နိုင်မလား။ |

## Sentence Forms

### Can

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I can go. | I can't go. | Can I go? | Can't I go? |
| He can help. | He can't help. | Can he help? | Can't he help? |

### Could

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I could go. | I couldn't go. | Could I go? | Couldn't I go? |
| He could help. | He couldn't help. | Could he help? | Couldn't he help? |

## Key Differences

| Feature | Can | Could |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Time | Present/Future | Past / Polite |
| Formality | Less formal | More formal/polite |
| Ability | Current ability | Past ability |
| Requests | Informal requests | Polite/formal requests |
