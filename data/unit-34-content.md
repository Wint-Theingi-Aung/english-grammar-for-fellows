# Unit 34: A / an, the, no article

## Learning Objectives
- Know when to use a/an with singular countable nouns
- Know when to use "the" for specific or previously mentioned things
- Know when to use no article for general statements

## A / an

Use a/an with singular, countable nouns when mentioning something for the first time or to describe profession.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| a before consonant sound | a bike, a teacher |
| an before vowel sound | an engineer, an apple |
| Profession | He is a teacher. She is an engineer. |

## The

Use "the" with countable (singular and plural) and uncountable nouns when referring to something previously mentioned, specific things, or unique things.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Previously mentioned | I have got a problem. The problem is not important. |
| Specific | The film I saw was good. |
| Unique things | the sun, the government, the world, the moon, the sea |

## No Article

Do not use an article with plural, countable nouns or uncountable nouns when talking about people or things in general.

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| General plural | I love oranges. |
| General uncountable | Education is important. |
| General statement | Music is relaxing. |
