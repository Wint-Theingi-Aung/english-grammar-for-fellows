# Unit 20: Too ... to

## Learning Objectives
- Use "too + adjective + to + V1" to express undesirable excess
- Use "too + adjective + for someone + to + V1" when the subject differs
- Combine two sentences into one using "too ... to"

## Too ... to

| Form | Usage |
|------|-------|
| too + adjective + to + V1 | Express undesirable excess (same subject) |
| too + adjective + for someone + to + V1 | Express undesirable excess (different subject) |

## Key Rules

| Rule | Example | Burmese |
|------|---------|---------|
| too + adj + to + V1 | The girl was too arrogant to listen to my advice. | မိန်းကလေးက ငါ့အကြံ နားမထောင်ဖို့ မန်းကလေး လွန်တယ်။ |
| too + adj + to + V1 | She was too tired to continue working. | သူမ အလုပ်ဆက်လုပ်ဖို့ အရမ်းပင်ပန်းနေပြီ။ |
| too + adj + for + person + to + V1 | These cars are too expensive for us to buy. | ကားတွေက ငါတို့ဝယ်ဖို့ တော်တော်ဈေးကြီးသတယ်။ |
| too + adj + for + person + to + V1 | Your story is too ridiculous for me to believe. | မင်းပြောပုံက ငါယုံဖို့ ရယ်စရာကောင်းလွန်တယ်။ |
