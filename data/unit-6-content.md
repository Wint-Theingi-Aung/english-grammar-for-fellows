# Unit 6: Will / Would

## Learning Objectives
- Understand when to use "will" and "would" in English sentences
- Form affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences with will/would
- Use will for future structures, promises, offers, and predictions
- Use would for past tense of will, conditional sentences, and polite requests

## Will / Would Overview

| Modal | Form | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|
| Will | Subject + will + V1 | Future, promise, offer, prediction, Type 1 conditional |
| Would | Subject + would + V1 | Past tense of will, Type 2/3 conditional, polite requests |

## Use 1: Will in Future Structures, Promises, Offers, and Predictions

"Will" is used in future structures and for promises, offers, and predictions.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| It will be Tuesday tomorrow. | မနက်ဖန် အင်္ဂလိပ်နေ့ ဖြစ်မယ်။ |
| We will have two extra lessons this afternoon. | ဒီနေ့လယ်ကျရင် ငါတို့ နောက်ထပ်သင်ခန်းစာ ၂ ခါ ရမယ်။ |
| That phone is ringing; I will go and answer it. | ဖုန်းလှမ်းနေတယ်။ ငါသွားကြည့်ပြီး ဖြေလိုက်မယ်။ |
| I will always love you. | ငါနင်ကို အမြဲချစ်မယ်။ |
| Don't trust her, she will always tell you lies. | သူမကို မယုံနဲ့။ သူမက မင်းကို အမြဲလိမ်နေတယ်။ |

## Use 2: Will in Type 1 Conditional Sentences

The word "will" is used in Type 1 conditional sentences (real/possible future conditions).

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| We will go fishing at the weekend if the weather is good. | ရာသီဥတု ကောင်းရင် ကျွန်တော်တို့ ငါးဖမ်းကြမယ်လေ။ |
| If you study well, you will pass the exam. | မင်း စာလေ့လာရင် စာမေးပွဲ အောင်မှာပါ။ |

## Use 3: Would as Past Tense of Will

"Would" is the past tense form of "will". It is used in reported speech and past narratives.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| Jack said he would finish the work the next day. | ဂျက်က သူ နောက်နေ့ ပြီးအောင်လုပ်မယ်လို့ ပြောခဲ့တယ်။ |
| Ann said she would write us soon. | အန်က သူမ မကြာခင် ငါတို့ဆီ စာရေးမယ် ပြောခဲ့တယ်။ |
| He hoped she would come back. | သူမပြန်လာမယ်လို့ သူ မျှော်လင့်ခဲ့တယ်။ |

## Use 4: Would in Type 2 and Type 3 Conditional Sentences

"Would" is used in Type 2 (unreal present) and Type 3 (unreal past) conditional sentences.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I would phone Sue if I had her number. | ငါမှာ သူ သူမဖုန်းနံပါတ်ရှိခဲ့ရင် ဆူကို ဆက်မှာမှာ ပါ။ |
| If the weather had been good, we would have gone fishing on the weekend. | ရာသီဥတု ကောင်းခဲ့ရင် ကျွန်တော်တို့ ဒီတစ်ပတ်ငါးဖမ်းကြမှာ ပါ။ |

## Use 5: Would for Polite Requests

"Would" is more polite than "will" and is used for polite requests and offers.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| Would you like some juice? | အခု ဖျော်ရည် သောက်ချင်လား။ |
| I would like to eat sandwich. | ကျွန်မ ဆန်းဒ်ဝိုက် စားချင်တယ်။ |

## Sentence Forms

### Will

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I will go. | I won't go. | Will I go? | Won't I go? |
| He will help. | He won't help. | Will he help? | Won't he help? |

### Would

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I would go. | I wouldn't go. | Would I go? | Wouldn't I go? |
| He would help. | He wouldn't help. | Would he help? | Wouldn't he help? |
