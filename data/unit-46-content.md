# Unit 46: Idioms

## Learning Objectives
- Learn common English idioms and their meanings
- Understand how idioms are used in everyday conversation
- Recognize idioms in context and use them appropriately

## Idioms

| # | Idiom | Meaning | Burmese |
|---|-------|---------|---------|
| 1 | walk of life | a person's occupation, status, or social class | လုပ်အလုပ် |
| 2 | a piece of cake | something very easy to do | အလွယ်တကူ |
| 3 | a penny for your thoughts | used to ask someone what they are thinking about | ဘာတွေ တွေးနေတာလဲ။ |
| 4 | all smiles | very happy and smiling | ပျော်ပန်းဝသည်။ |
| 5 | black sheep | a person who is considered a disgrace or failure by their family or group | စိတ်နှောင့်ရှက် |
| 6 | break the ice | to initiate a conversation or ease tension in a social situation | ရင်ကြား သိ အစပျိုး စကားစပြသည်။ |
| 7 | child's play | something very easy to do | အလွန်လွယ်ကူသောအရာ |
| 8 | crocodile tears | fake or insincere tears; pretending to be sad | ဟန်ဆောင်မဲက်ရည် |
| 9 | foot the bill | to pay for something; to cover the cost | ဒီကုန်ကျသည်၊ ကုန်ကျစရိတ်ခံသည်။ |
| 10 | get rid of | to remove or throw away something unwanted | ပထွေးသည်၊ ဖယ်ရှားသည်၊ ရှင်းပစ်သည်။ |
| 11 | get a grip | to control oneself or a situation; to calm down | အားတင်းသည်။ |
| 12 | go through the roof | to become extremely angry | အဒေါသပေါက်ကွဲသည်။ |
| 13 | I'm afraid | used to politely express that something is not possible or to deliver bad news | အားနာပါရစေ၊ စိတ်မကောင်းလို့တဲ့ |
| 14 | leave it at that | to stop talking about something; to accept the current situation | ဒီလောက်ပဲ ထားလိုက်ကြစို့ |
| 15 | not at all | a polite response to thanks; not in any way | ကျေးဇူးမပြုပါဘူး။ ရပါတယ်။ |
| 16 | on the spot | immediately; right away; at that very moment | ချက်ချင်း၊ ပဲ့ခင်းတင် |
| 17 | off colour | feeling unwell or slightly ill | နမကောင်းဖြစ်သည်။ |
| 18 | over the moon | extremely happy and delighted | များကျေနပ်ဝမ်းမြောက်လှကြသည်။ |
| 19 | once in a blue moon | very rarely; almost never | တစ်လကို သီတင်းကွတ်မှ တစ်ကြိမ် |
| 20 | out of the blue | unexpectedly; without warning | မမျှော်လင့်ဘဲဖြစ်လာသည်။ |
| 21 | pie in the sky | an unrealistic or impossible hope or plan | မရနိုင်သောမျှော်လင့်ချက် |

## Examples

### 1. walk of life
- She has friends from all walks of life.
- Our volunteers include people from all walks of life.
- A doctor and a street cleaner are in different walks of life.

### 2. a piece of cake
- The exam was a piece of cake.
- Creating graphs is a piece of cake on the computer.
- No sweat. This project will be a piece of cake.

### 3. a penny for your thoughts
- "You have been quiet all morning, a penny for your thoughts?"
- You seem to be thinking very hard about something. A penny for your thoughts.
- Right now I use Bitcoin to dream. And it's better than a penny for your thoughts. You get better dreams.

### 4. all smiles
- The worried look disappeared from her face and she was all smiles again.
- She was all smiles at the news of her win.
- He was all smiles at the good news.

### 5. black sheep
- My brother is the black sheep of the family.
- Every family has a black sheep.
- He was the black sheep of the family after he had got involved in the bank robbery.

### 6. break the ice
- She sent him a text to break the ice.
- She helps break the ice when I am interviewing.
- Nobody can break the ice with him.

### 7. child's play
- The test was real child's play.
- Driving a car is not child's play.
- Riding a bicycle is child's play when you've had some practice.

### 8. crocodile tears
- You mean she was crying crocodile tears?
- Don't soften your heart; he was shedding crocodile tears.
- There were only crying crocodile tears at the old man's funeral.

### 9. foot the bill
- Who's going to foot the bill for the damage?
- You will have to foot the bill for any treatment.
- Let me foot the bill.

### 10. get rid of
- It is difficult to get rid of insects.
- I can't get rid of the idea.
- I can't get rid of this headache.

### 11. get a grip
- Get a grip on yourself!
- Some people need to get a grip on themselves.
- Some might say, "Oh, get a grip!"

### 12. go through the roof
- Mom will go through the roof when she finds out we disobeyed her again.
- When they realized he'd lied to them, his parents went through the roof.
- She went through the roof when I told her I'd crashed her car.

### 13. I'm afraid
- I'm afraid we can't come.
- I'm afraid the doctor is busy at the moment. Can he call you back?
- I'm afraid I am late for the train.

### 14. leave it at that
- My daughter's new boyfriend is one year older than me. Let's just leave it at that.
- We will never agree, so let's just leave it at that.
- If you are not sure of the answers, you leave it at that.

### 15. not at all
- "Thank you for all your help." "Not at all."
- He is not at all worried about his car's reliability.
- My English teacher often replies as "not at all" – when we say "thank you" to her.

### 16. on the spot
- The policeman arrested the thief on the spot.
- He answered the question on the spot.
- He had to make a decision on the spot.

### 17. off colour
- You look a little off colour today.
- I am fine, thank you, but John is a bit off colour at the moment.
- You are off colour.

### 18. over the moon
- She's over the moon about her new job.
- They are over the moon about their trip to Japan.
- She is over the moon about the two holidays.

### 19. once in a blue moon
- I see her once in a blue moon.
- That kind of chance comes once in a blue moon.
- Such a chance comes once in a blue moon.

### 20. out of the blue
- She arrived out of the blue.
- His resignation came out of the blue.
- It was totally out of the blue.

### 21. pie in the sky
- It's pie in the sky.
- Your idea is just pie in the sky.
- His dream to become President is just a pie in the sky.
