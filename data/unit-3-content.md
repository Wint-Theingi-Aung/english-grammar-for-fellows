# Unit 3: Verb to Have (has, have, had)

## Learning Objectives
- Understand and use the verb "have" as both a main verb and an auxiliary verb
- Form sentences with "have" for ownership and possession
- Use "have" as an auxiliary verb in Present Perfect, Past Perfect, and Future Perfect tenses
- Form affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences
- Give short answers using "have" forms

## Verb Forms Overview

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Simple Present (I/we/you/they) | have | I have a car. |
| Simple Present (he/she/it) | has | She has many pencils. |
| Simple Past | had | I had a car before. |
| Simple Future | will have | I will have a car next year. |

## Subject-Verb Conjugation Table

| Subject | Present | Past | Future |
|---------|---------|------|--------|
| I | have | had | will have |
| We | have | had | will have |
| You | have | had | will have |
| He | has | had | will have |
| She | has | had | will have |
| It | has | had | will have |
| They | have | had | will have |

## Main Verb — Ownership/Possession

### Example Sentences

- I have a car. (ကျွန်မ ကားတစ်စီး ရှိတယ်။)
- I had a car before. (အရင်က ကျွန်မ ကားတစ်စီး ရှိခဲ့တယ်။)
- I will have a car next year. (နောက်နှစ် ကျွန်မ ကားတစ်စီး ရှိမယ်။)
- We have lots of books.
- He has his own diary.
- She has many pencils.
- They have many clothes.

### Sentence Forms

#### Simple Present

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I have a car. | I don't have a car. | Do I have a car? | Don't I have a car? |

#### Simple Past

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|
| I had a car before. | I didn't have a car before. | Did I have a car before? |

#### Simple Future

| Affirmative | Negative |
|-------------|----------|
| I will have a car next year. | I won't have a car next year. |

## Auxiliary Verb — Perfect Tenses

### Present Perfect

Form: Subject + have/has + V3

- I have walked on this path before.
- He has written several movie scripts.
- She has been here for twenty minutes.
- They have visited Florida several times.

### Past Perfect

Form: Subject + had + V3

- I had seen him before.
- We had met him before he became famous.
- She had established her company before 2008.
- Tom had copied his document before the computer crashed.

### Future Perfect

Form: Subject + will have + V3

- I will have left for my trip.
- We will have finished our project.
- You will have sent the document by Tuesday.
- She will have joined the meeting before you reach.

## Short Answers

### Simple Present

- Do you have a car? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

### Simple Past

- Did you have a car before? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

### Simple Future

- Will you have a car next year? → Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

### Present Perfect

- Has she been here for twenty minutes? → Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.

### Past Perfect

- Had he met her before the party? → Yes, he had. / No, he hadn't.

### Future Perfect

- Will they have finished by tomorrow? → Yes, they will. / No, they won't.
