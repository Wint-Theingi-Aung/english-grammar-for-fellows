# Unit 30: As soon as

## Learning Objectives
- Learn to join two sentences using "as soon as"
- Replace "when", "at once", "immediately" with "as soon as"
- Preserve correct tense sequence when rewriting sentences

## As soon as

| Form | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| As soon as | As soon as + clause (first action), main clause (second action) |

"As soon as" joins two sentences to show that the second action happens immediately after the first. Write "as soon as" before the clause that shows the first action.

## Key Examples

| Two Sentences | Combined with As soon as |
|---------------|--------------------------|
| He received the telegram. He started at once. | As soon as he received the telegram, he started. |
| When the food arrived, he dug in hungrily. | As soon as the food arrived, he dug in hungrily. |
| He started his car as the driver saw green signal. | He started his car as soon as the driver saw the green signal. |

## Attention
If a sentence already contains "as", "when", "at once", "immediately", or "no sooner", remove it and put "as soon as" in its place, then connect the other sentence with a comma.
