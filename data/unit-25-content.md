# Unit 25: Double Comparatives (…တစ်လေ …တစ်လေ)

## Learning Objectives
- Learn to form double comparatives using "the + comparative, the + comparative"
- Express cause and effect relationships using double comparatives
- Transform simple sentences into double comparative structures

## Double Comparatives

| Form | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| Double Comparative | The + comparative + subject + verb, the + comparative + subject + verb |

## Key Examples

| Simple Sentence | Double Comparative |
|----------------|-------------------|
| He eats more meat. He is fatter. | The more meat he eats, the fatter he is. |
| The sun is high. The shadow is low. | The higher the sun is, the lower the shadow is. |
| He worked hard. He became rich. | The harder he worked, the richer he became. |
