# Unit 2: Verb to Be (am, is, are, was, were)

## Learning Objectives
- Understand and use the verb "be" (am, is, are, was, were) as a main verb and auxiliary verb
- Form affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences with "be"
- Use the correct form of "be" for different subjects (I, we, you, he, she, it, they)
- Understand the different complement types used with "be"
- Use short and long answer forms with "be"

## Verb "Be" Overview

The verb "be" is used as a main verb and as an auxiliary verb.

### Main Verb Usage
When used as a main verb, "be" connects the subject to a complement:

| Complement Type | Structure | Example |
|-----------------|-----------|---------|
| Noun Complement | Subject + be + noun | I am a doctor. |
| Place Complement | Subject + be + place | I am here. |
| Adjective Complement | Subject + be + adjective | I am beautiful. |
| Present Participle (Ving) | Subject + be + Ving | I am writing a letter. |
| Past Participle (V3) | Subject + be + V3 | I am hit by teacher. |

### Auxiliary Verb Usage
"Be" is used as an auxiliary verb to form continuous tenses and passive voice.

## Subject-Verb Conjugation Table

| Subject | Simple Present | Simple Past | Simple Future |
|---------|----------------|-------------|---------------|
| I | am | was | will be |
| We | are | were | will be |
| You | are | were | will be |
| He | is | was | will be |
| She | is | was | will be |
| It | is | was | will be |
| They | are | were | will be |

## Example Sentences

### Simple Present Tense
- I am a student. (am = verb "be" with "I")
  - ကျွန်မ ကျောင်းသားတစ်ယောက်ပါ။
- We are in the park. (are = verb "be" with "we")
  - ငါတို့ ပန်းခြံထဲမှာ ရှိတယ်။
- You are my friend. (are = verb "be" with "you")
  - မင်း ငါ့သူငယ်ချင်းပါ။
- He is a teacher. (is = verb "be" with "he")
  - သူ ဆရာတစ်ယောက်ပါ။
- She is beautiful. (is = verb "be" with "she")
  - သူမ လှပတယ်။
- It is rainy. (is = verb "be" with "it")
  - မိုးရွာနေတယ်။
- They are doctors. (are = verb "be" with "they")
  - သူတို့ ဆရာဝန်တွေပါ။

### Simple Past Tense
- I was at home last night. (was = verb "be" with "I")
  - မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ အိမ်မှာ ရှိခဲ့တယ်။
- We were at school yesterday. (were = verb "be" with "we")
  - မနေ့က ငါတို့ ကျောင်းမှာ ရှိခဲ့တယ်။
- You were happy yesterday. (were = verb "be" with "you")
  - မနေ့က မင်း ပျော်ရွှင်ခဲ့တယ်။
- He was a student two years ago. (was = verb "be" with "he")
  - နှစ်နှစ်ကျော်က သူ ကျောင်းသားတစ်ယောက်ပါ။
- She was tired last night. (was = verb "be" with "she")
  - မနေ့ညက သူမ ပင်ပန်းနေတယ်။
- It was cold yesterday. (was = verb "be" with "it")
  - မနေ့က အအေးချမ်းတယ်။
- They were at the market last week. (were = verb "be" with "they")
  - ပြီးခဲ့တဲ့အပတ်က သူတို့ စျေးမှာ ရှိခဲ့တယ်။

### Simple Future Tense
- I will be a doctor next year. (will be = verb "be" with "I")
  - နောက်နှစ် ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်ဖြစ်မယ်။
- We will be at home tomorrow. (will be = verb "be" with "we")
  - မနက်ဖန် ငါတို့ အိမ်မှာ ရှိမယ်။
- You will be late next time. (will be = verb "be" with "you")
  - နောက်တစ်ကြိမ် မင်း နောက်ကျမယ်။
- He will be here soon. (will be = verb "be" with "he")
  - သူ မကြာခင် ရောက်လာမယ်။
- She will be happy tomorrow. (will be = verb "be" with "she")
  - မနက်ဖန် သူမ ပျော်ရွှင်မယ်။
- It will be hot next week. (will be = verb "be" with "it")
  - နောက်အပတ် ပူနွေးမယ်။
- They will be at school next Monday. (will be = verb "be" with "they")
  - နောက်တနင်္လာနေ့ သူတို့ ကျောင်းမှာ ရှိမယ်။

## Sentence Forms

"Be" is used to make affirmative, negative, interrogative, and negative interrogative sentences.

### Affirmative, Negative, Interrogative, and Negative Interrogative Forms

#### Simple Present Tense

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I am a doctor. | I am not a doctor. | Am I a doctor? | Am I not a doctor? |
| ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်တစ်ယောက်ပါ။ | ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်မဟုတ်ဘူး။ | ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်လား။ | ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်မဟုတ်ဘူးလား။ |

#### Simple Past Tense

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I was at home last night. | I was not at home last night. | Was I at home last night? | Was I not at home last night? |
| မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ အိမ်မှာ ရှိခဲ့တယ်။ | မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ အိမ်မှာ မရှိခဲ့ဘူး။ | မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ အိမ်မှာ ရှိခဲ့လား။ | မနေ့ညက ကျွန်မ အိမ်မှာ မရှိခဲ့ဘူးလား။ |

#### Simple Future Tense

| Affirmative | Negative | Interrogative | Negative Interrogative |
|-------------|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| I will be a doctor next year. | I won't be a doctor next year. | Will I be a doctor next year? | Won't I be a doctor next year? |
| နောက်နှစ် ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်ဖြစ်မယ်။ | နောက်နှစ် ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်မဖြစ်ဘူး။ | နောက်နှစ် ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်ဖြစ်မလား။ | နောက်နှစ် ကျွန်မ ဆရာဝန်မဖြစ်ဘူးလား။ |

## Answer Forms (Short and Long Answers)

When we are replying Yes/No Questions with "be", we can use short or long form for the answer.

### Examples:

**Am I a doctor?**
- Short Answer: Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
- Long Answer: Yes, you are a doctor. / No, you aren't a doctor.

**Is he a teacher?**
- Short Answer: Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, he is a teacher. / No, he isn't a teacher.

**Is she beautiful?**
- Short Answer: Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
- Long Answer: Yes, she is beautiful. / No, she isn't beautiful.

**Are we in the park?**
- Short Answer: Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
- Long Answer: Yes, we are in the park. / No, we aren't in the park.

**Are you my friend?**

- Long Answer: Yes, I am your friend. / No, I'm not your friend.

**Are they doctors?**
