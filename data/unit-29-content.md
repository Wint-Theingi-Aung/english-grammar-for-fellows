# Unit 29: Not only … but also

## Learning Objectives
- Learn to connect and emphasize two phrases using "not only … but also"
- Understand parallelism with "not only … but also"
- Maintain subject-verb agreement with "not only … but also"

## Not only … but also

| Form | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| With nouns/adjectives/adverbs | Subject + verb + not only + noun/adj/adv + but also + noun/adj/adv |
| With verbs | Subject + not only + verb + but also + verb |

"Not only … but also" connects and emphasizes two phrases or words at the same position. Parallelism should be the goal.

## Key Examples

| Two Sentences | Combined with Not only … but also |
|---------------|-----------------------------------|
| He can speak English. He can speak German. | He can speak not only English but also German. |
| I like cheesecake. I like cake. | I like not only cheesecake but also cake. |
| He is handsome. He is also intelligent. | He is not only handsome but also intelligent. |
| He speaks Spanish naturally. He speaks Spanish fluently. | He speaks Spanish not only naturally but also fluently. |

## Attention
If one subject is singular and the other is plural, the verb form agrees with the subject that is nearest the verb.
