# Unit 35: Relative Pronouns

## Learning Objectives
- Use "who" and "that" for people
- Use "which" and "that" for things
- Use "whose" for possessions
- Use "where" for places
- Use "when" for times
- Know when the relative pronoun can be omitted

## Relative Pronouns

Relative pronouns give information about the person, thing, place, or time in the first half of the sentence.

## Who and that for people

| Two Sentences | Combined Sentence |
|---------------|-------------------|
| We met a girl. The girl had lost her way. | We met a girl who had lost her way. |
| I saw a soldier. He had lost an arm. | I saw a soldier who had lost an arm. |
| The boy solved the puzzle. He was praised by the teacher. | The boy who solved the puzzle was praised by the teacher. |
| The person phoned. He didn't leave a message. | The person who phoned didn't leave a message. |

## Which and that for things

| Two Sentences | Combined Sentence |
|---------------|-------------------|
| She lives in New York. She likes living in New York. | She lives in New York which she likes. |
| Bring me the file. The file is on the table. | Bring me the file which is on the table. |
| I bought a new car. It is very fast. | I bought a new car that is very fast. |

## Whose for possessions

| Two Sentences | Combined Sentence |
|---------------|-------------------|
| The dog is over there. Its owner lives next door. | The dog whose owner lives next door is over there. |
| This is the man. His dog bit me. | This is the man whose dog bit me. |
| I have a boyfriend. His father is a famous actor. | I have a boyfriend whose father is a famous actor. |

## Where for places

| Two Sentences | Combined Sentence |
|---------------|-------------------|
| I know a great restaurant. We can get good food. | I know a great restaurant where we can get good food. |
| The office is a place. I can do my work without being disturbed. | The office is a place where I can do my work without being disturbed. |
| There is the cafeteria. We can have lunch. | There is the cafeteria where we can have lunch. |

## When for times

| Two Sentences | Combined Sentence |
|---------------|-------------------|
| My parents remember that time. There was no internet. | My parents remember that time when there was no internet. |
| My sister was born at a time. My dad went abroad. | My sister was born at a time when my dad went abroad. |
| I will never forget the day. I got my first bike on that day. | I will never forget the day when I got my first bike. |

## Attention

We can omit who, which, or that when a noun or pronoun comes immediately after.

- That's the film that I saw. = That's the film (that) I saw.
- BUT: That's the film that was popular. (Cannot omit "that" because it is the subject)
