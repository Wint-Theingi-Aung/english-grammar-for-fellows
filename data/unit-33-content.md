# Unit 33: Nouns: Countable and Uncountable

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the difference between countable and uncountable nouns
- Learn to identify which nouns are countable and which are uncountable

## Countable Nouns

Countable nouns can be counted. They can be singular or plural.

**Examples:** an apple, two apples, three apples / a child, two children

## Uncountable Nouns

Uncountable nouns cannot be counted. They do not have a plural form.

**Examples:** air, rice, water, milk, butter, glue, juice, oil

## Key Differences

| Countable | Uncountable |
|-----------|-------------|
| an apple | water |
| two books | information |
| three children | furniture |
| many windows | some rice |
