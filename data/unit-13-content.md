# Unit 13: Seem / Seem to

## Learning Objectives
- Use "seem" with adjectives to describe appearances
- Use "seem to" before verbs to express how things appear
- Form negatives and questions with "seem" and "seem to"

## Seem / Seem to Overview

| Form | Usage |
|------|-------|
| Subject + seem + Adjective | Describing how someone/something appears |
| Subject + seem to + V1 | Expressing how something appears to happen |

## Use 1: Seem + Adjective

"Seem" is used with adjectives to describe how someone or something appears.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| You seem happy. | မင်း ပျော်ပျော်ပါရတယ်။ |
| He seems kind. | သူ ကောင်းနပါရတယ်။ |

## Use 2: Seem to + V1

"Seem to" is used before a verb infinitive to express how something appears to happen.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| You seem to know it. | မင်း အဲဒီကို သိပါရတယ်။ |
| She doesn't seem to work there. | သူမ အမှန် အလုပ်လုပ်ပုံ မပေါ်ဘူး။ |
| They don't seem to recognize me. | သူတို့ ငါ့ကို မမှတ်မိပုံ မပေါ်ဘူး။ |
