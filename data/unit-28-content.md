# Unit 28: Both … and

## Learning Objectives
- Learn to combine two sentences using "both … and"
- Understand subject-verb agreement with "both … and"
- Use "both … and" to connect nouns, adjectives, and verbs

## Both … and

| Form | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| Both … and | Both + noun/adjective + and + noun/adjective |

"Both … and" refers to two things or people together. It is always considered **plural** in a sentence.

## Key Examples

| Two Sentences | Combined with Both … and |
|---------------|--------------------------|
| You can be mother. You can be business woman. | You can be both mother and business woman. |
| Alice has to comply with the rules. Susan has to comply with the rules. | Both Alice and Susan have to comply with the rules. |
| The students are in the class. The teacher is in the class. | Both the students and the teacher are in the class. |
| Sue is kind to others. Sue is helpful to others. | Sue is both kind and helpful to others. |

## Attention
"Both … and" always takes a plural verb, even if one of the subjects is singular.
