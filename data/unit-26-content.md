# Unit 26: Either … or

## Learning Objectives
- Learn to use "either … or" to connect alternatives
- Understand subject-verb agreement with "either … or"
- Combine two sentences using "either … or"

## Either … or

| Form | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| Either … or | Either + noun/clause + or + noun/clause |

## Key Examples

| Two Sentences | Combined with Either … or |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| He cooks dinner. Or she cooks dinner. | Either he or she cooks dinner. |
| He washes the dishes. Or he irons the clothes. | He either washes the dishes or irons the clothes. |
| My parents work hard. Or my brother works hard. | Either my parents or my brother works hard to support the family. |

## Attention
If one subject is singular and the other is plural, the verb agrees with the subject nearest to it.
