# Unit 16: Going to

## Learning Objectives
- Use "going to" to talk about future plans and intentions
- Use "going to" to make predictions based on present evidence
- Form positives, negatives, and questions with "going to"

## Going to Overview

| Form | Usage |
|------|-------|
| Subject + am/is/are + going to + V1 | Future plans and intentions |
| Subject + am/is/are + going to + V1 | Predictions based on evidence |

## Use 1: Going to for Future Plans

"Going to" is used to talk about plans and intentions for the future.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I am going to play football with my friends. | ငါ သူငယ်ချင်းတွေနဲ့ ဘောလုံး ကစားတော့မယ်။ |
| The baby is going to sleep. | ကလေး အိပ်တော့မယ်။ |

## Use 2: Going to for Predictions

"Going to" is used to make predictions based on what we can see now.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I think I am going to be sick. | ငါ အန်မယ်ဖြစ်တော့မယ်လို့ ထင်တယ်။ |
| Are you going to take a bath? | မင်း ရေချိုးတော့မလား လဲ။ |

## Important Notes
- "Going to" is used for future plans we have already decided
- "Going to" is used for predictions when we can see evidence now
- The structure is: Subject + am/is/are + going to + base verb (V1)
