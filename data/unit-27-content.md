# Unit 27: Neither … nor

## Learning Objectives
- Learn to use "neither … nor" to connect negative alternatives
- Understand subject-verb agreement with "neither … nor"
- Combine two negative sentences using "neither … nor"

## Neither … nor

| Form | Pattern |
|------|---------|
| Neither … nor | Neither + noun/verb/phrase + nor + noun/verb/phrase |

## Key Examples

| Two Negative Sentences | Combined with Neither … nor |
|------------------------|----------------------------|
| I don't like banana. I don't like apple. | I like neither banana nor apple. |
| He does not drink. He does not smoke. | He neither drinks nor smokes. |
| I am not watching the movie on TV. I am not watching on my laptop. | I am watching the movie neither on TV nor on my laptop. |

## Attention
If one subject is singular and the other is plural, the verb agrees with the subject nearest to it.
