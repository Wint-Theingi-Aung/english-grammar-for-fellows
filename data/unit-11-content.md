# Unit 11: Have to

## Learning Objectives
- Understand when to use "have to" in English sentences
- Use "have to" for responsibilities in work and everyday life
- Use "don't have to" to express that something is not required
- Understand that "have to" is used in past, present, and future

## Have to Overview

| Form | Usage |
|------|-------|
| Subject + have to + V1 | Responsibilities (present/future) |
| Subject + don't/doesn't have to + V1 | Not required (present/future) |
| Subject + had to + V1 | Responsibilities (past) |
| Did + subject + have to + V1? | Questions about past obligations |

## Use 1: Have to for Responsibilities

"Have to" is used for responsibilities at work and in everyday life. It is conjugated as a regular verb and requires an auxiliary verb in questions and negatives.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I have to write a letter to John. | ကျွန်တော် ဂျွန်ဆီ စာတစ်စောင် ရေးစရာရှိတယ်။ |
| Mike can't come because he has to work tomorrow. | မိုက် အလပ်လပ်စရာရှိလို့ သူ မလာနိုင်ဘူး။ |
| Children have to go to school. | ကလေးများ ကျောင်းသွားရမယ်။ |
| He has to see the doctor. | သူ ဆရာဝန်ဆီ သွားရမယ်။ |
| Susan and Steve have to pay their rent every Friday. | ဆူဇန်နဲ့ စတိဗ်တို့ အိမ်ငှားခကို အပတ်တိုင်း သောကြာနေ့မှာ ပေးရမယ်။ |
| I have to file reports every week. | ငါ အပတ်တိုင်း အစီရင်ခံစာ တင်ရမယ်။ |

## Use 2: Don't Have to for Not Required

"Don't have to" is used to express that something is not required or not necessary.

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| I don't have to work on Sundays so I can do what I want. | ကျွန်တော် တနင်္ဂနွေနေ့တွေ အလပ်မရဘူး။ ဒါကြောင့် ငါ ဘာလုပ်ချင်တာလုပ်နိုင်တယ်။ |
| She doesn't have to come to the meeting if she doesn't want to. | အစည်းအဝေး မလာချင်ရင် မလာပါနဲ့။ |
| You don't have to drive today. | မင်း ဒီနေ့ မောင်းစရာ မလိုဘူး။ |
| She doesn't have to learn your language. | သူမ မင်းဘာသာစကား သင်စရာ မလိုဘူး။ |
| You don't have to drive. I can do it. | မင်း မောင်းစရာ မလိုဘူး။ ငါ မောင်းနိုင်တယ်။ |

## Use 3: Have to in Past, Present, and Future

"Have to" is used in all three tenses. The past form is "had to".

| Tense | Form | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Present | have to / has to | I have to work. |
| Past | had to | I had to work yesterday. |
| Future | have to | I will have to work tomorrow. |

| Example | Burmese |
|---------|---------|
| She had to pick the children up from school yesterday. | သူမ မနေ့က ကလေးတွေကို ကျောင်းကနေ ခေါ်လာခရတယ်။ |
| Did he have to leave so early? | သူ အစောစောထွက်လိုက်ရတာလား။ |
| I had to go to the dentist yesterday. | ငါ မနေ့က သွားဆရာဝန်ဆီ သွားရတယ်။ |
| Did they have to do the work over again? | သူတို့ အလုပ်ကို ပြန်လုပ်ရတာလား။ |
