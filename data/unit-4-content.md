# Unit 4: Question Tags

## Learning Objectives
- Understand what question tags are and how they are used
- Form correct question tags for positive statements (negative tags)
- Form correct question tags for negative statements (positive tags)
- Use question tags with different tenses (present, past, future, perfect, modals)
- Handle special cases and exceptions (imperative, let's, nobody/nothing, "I am", "have" as main verb)

## What Are Question Tags?

A question tag is a short question added to the end of a statement. It is used to confirm information or ask for agreement.

- Question tag ဆိုတာ စကားရပ်ရဲ့ နောက်ဆုံးမှာ ထည့်တဲ့ မေးခွန်းတိုလေးပါ။ အချက်အလက်ကို အတည်ပြုဖို့ သို့မဟုတ် သဘောတူညီမှုကို မေးဖို့ သုံးပါတယ်။

## Rules for Forming Question Tags

### Rule 1: The sentence and question tag must be in the same tense
- ဝါကျနဲ့ question tag က အချိန်ကာလတူရပါမယ်။

### Rule 2: Always use pronouns in the question tag
- Question tag မှာ အမြဲတမ်း pronoun ကိုသုံးပါ။

### Rule 3: Positive statement → negative question tag
- အတည်ပြုဝါကျ → ငြင်းဆို question tag

### Rule 4: Negative statement → positive question tag
- ငြင်းဆိုဝါကျ → အတည်ပြု question tag

### Rule 5: The auxiliary verb in the tag matches the main verb tense
- Question tag ရဲ့ auxiliary verb က main verb ရဲ့ အချိန်ကာလနဲ့ ကိုက်ညီရပါမယ်။

## Question Tags by Tense

### Simple Present Tense

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Positive (V1/V1+s) | don't/doesn't + pronoun | You play the guitar, don't you? |
| Negative (don't/doesn't + V1) | do/does + pronoun | You don't go there, do you? |

- ပုံမှန်အတည်ပြု: You play the guitar, don't you?
  - မင်း ဂစ်တာ တီးတယ်၊ မတီးဘူးလား?
- ပုံမှန်ငြင်းဆို: You don't go there, do you?
  - မင်း ဒီမှာ မသွားဘူး၊ သွားလား?
- He/She/It: Sue likes tennis, doesn't she?
  - Sue က တင်းနစ် ကြိုက်တယ်၊ မကြိုက်ဘူးလား?
- He/She/It negative: She doesn't iron the clothes, does she?
  - သူမ အဝတ် မညှစ်ဘူး၊ ညှစ်လား?

### Simple Past Tense

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Positive (V2) | didn't + pronoun | You went there, didn't you? |
| Negative (didn't + V1) | did + pronoun | You didn't do your homework, did you? |

- အတိတ်အတည်ပြု: You went there, didn't you?
  - မင်း ဒီမှာ သွားခဲ့တယ်၊ မသွားခဲ့ဘူးလား?
- အတိတ်ငြင်းဆို: You didn't do your homework, did you?
  - မင်း အိမ်စာ မလုပ်ခဲ့ဘူး၊ လုပ်ခဲ့လား?
- He/She/It: John knew about this letter, didn't he?
  - John က ဒီစာအကြောင်း သိတယ်၊ မသိဘူးလား?

### Simple Future Tense

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Positive (will + V1) | won't + pronoun | You will pass the exam, won't you? |
| Negative (won't + V1) | will + pronoun | You won't fail the exam, will you? |

- အနာဂတ်အတည်ပြု: You will pass the exam, won't you?
  - မင်း စာမေးပွဲ အောင်မယ်၊ အောင်မှာမဟုတ်ဘူးလား?
- အနာဂတ်ငြင်းဆို: You won't fail the exam, will you?
  - မင်း စာမေးပွဲ မရှုံးဘူး၊ ရှုံးလား?

### Present Perfect Tense

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Positive (have/has + V3) | haven't/hasn't + pronoun | You have studied all week, haven't you? |
| Negative (haven't/hasn't + V3) | have/has + pronoun | You haven't been here all week, have you? |

- Present Perfect အတည်ပြု: You have studied all week, haven't you?
  - မင်း တစ်ပတ်လုံး စာလေ့လာခဲ့တယ်၊ မလေ့လာခဲ့ဘူးလား?
- Present Perfect ငြင်းဆို: She hasn't written a letter, has she?
  - သူမ စာ မရေးခဲ့ဘူး၊ ရေးခဲ့လား?

### Past Perfect Tense

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Positive (had + V3) | hadn't + pronoun | You had arrived before he left, hadn't you? |
| Negative (hadn't + V3) | had + pronoun | You hadn't done it before, had you? |

- Past Perfect အတည်ပြု: You had arrived before he left, hadn't you?
  - သူ မထွက်ခင် မင်း ရောက်ပြီးပြီ၊ မရောက်ခင်ဘူးလား?

### Modal Verbs

| Statement | Tag | Example |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| Positive (can/must/should/would + V1) | can't/mustn't/shouldn't/wouldn't + pronoun | Mary can speak English, can't she? |
| Negative (can't/mustn't/shouldn't/wouldn't + V1) | can/must/should/would + pronoun | John can't drive a car, can he? |

- Modal အတည်ပြု: Mary can speak English, can't she?
  - Mary က အင်္ဂလိပ် ပြောတတ်တယ်၊ မပြောတတ်ဘူးလား?
- Modal အတည်ပြု: We must be patient, mustn't we?
  - ငါတို့ စိတ်ရှည်ရပါမယ်၊ မရှည်ရဘူးလား?
- Modal ငြင်းဆို: He shouldn't say things like that, should he?
  - သူ ဒီလို စကားမျိုး မပြောသင့်ဘူး၊ ပြောသင့်လား?

## Special Cases and Exceptions

### "I am" → "aren't I?"
- I am attractive, aren't I?
  - ကျွန်မ ဆွဲဆောင်မှု ရှိတယ်၊ မရှိဘူးလား?
- I am not the person with the tickets, am I?
  - ကျွန်မ လက်မှတ်ပိုင်ရှင် မဟုတ်ဘူး၊ ဟုတ်လား?

### Imperative → "will you?"
- Close the window, will you?
  - ပြတင်းပေါက် ပိတ်ပါ၊ ပိတ်မလား?
- Open the door, will you?
  - တံခါး ဖွင့်ပါ၊ ဖွင့်မလား?

### Let's → "shall we?"
- Let's go to the beach, shall we?
  - ကမ်းခြေသွားကြရအောင်၊ သွားကြမလား?
- Let's have lunch, shall we?
  - နေ့လည်စာ စားကြရအောင်၊ စားကြမလား?

### Nobody/Nothing → positive tag with "they/it"
- Nobody lives in this house, do they?
  - ဘယ်သူမှ ဒီအိမ်မှာ မနေဘူး၊ နေကြလား?
- Nothing bad happened, did it?
  - ဘာမဆိုးတာ မဖြစ်ခဲ့ဘူး၊ ဖြစ်ခဲ့လား?

### "Have" as main verb → "do/does"
- You have a Ferrari, don't you?
  - မင်းမှာ Ferrari ရှိတယ်၊ မရှိဘူးလား?
- You had a great time, didn't you?
  - မင်း အချိန်ကောင်းကောင်း ဖြတ်သန်းခဲ့တယ်၊ မဖြတ်သန်းခဲ့ဘူးလား?

### "There" sentences
- There aren't any spiders, are there?
  - ပိတ်ကျား ဘာမှ မရှိဘူး၊ ရှိလား?
- There are some bees, aren't there?
  - ပျားတွေ ရှိတယ်၊ မရှိဘူးလား?

### This is → "isn't it?"
- This is Paul's pen, isn't it?
  - ဒါ Paul ရဲ့ ဘောပင်ပါ၊ မဟုတ်ဘူးလား?
